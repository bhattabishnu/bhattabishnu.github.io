Background and Academic Direction

My academic background combines Electrical Engineering with a second undergraduate degree in Liberal Arts and Natural Sciences. This interdisciplinary education has shaped my interest in understanding technology not only through its technical foundations, but also through its social, ethical, and philosophical implications.

My engineering studies introduced me to mathematical modelling, simulation, and electrical systems. My Liberal Arts education developed my interests in philosophical inquiry, ethics, and independent research and writing.

I am currently developing my foundations in Natural Language Processing and AI, with the aim of contributing to research on responsible and accountable AI systems.
