Profile

My academic experience spans philosophical and ethical inquiry, interdisciplinary humanities education, and foundational training in electrical engineering. My undergraduate work included a thesis on electrical-grid frequency stabilisation using a Virtual Synchronous Machine (VSM), involving MATLAB/Simulink modelling and simulation. I also completed independent research and Senior thesis writing during my second undergraduate degree in liberal arts.

I am now strengthening my computational and NLP foundations while exploring how ethical and philosophical approaches can inform the design, evaluation, and governance of AI systems.
